Senv

Private chats, helpful agents.

Senv is an end-to-end encrypted messenger for family and friends, with agents that help you follow through.

Private by design

  • Messages, voice notes, reactions and receipts are end-to-end encrypted with MLS, the IETF standard for secure group messaging.
  • Our server only passes along encrypted messages it cannot read, and deletes them once delivered or after 30 days at most.
  • People find you by your handle only. Your phone number is never shown, searchable or shared.

Agents that follow through

Senv Assistant runs on your iPhone with Apple's on-device model. For bigger jobs, Senv Agent works in its own isolated workspace and shows you what it is doing.
